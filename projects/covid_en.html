Spreading Covid19

About the Game

In this game, you play as the first person infected with Covid-19. Your goal is to infect 10 people in your country, then travel to other countries to infect people more people.

Story of the Game

The story of this game began in 2020, when I was living in Australia during the Covid-19 pandemic. When school shut down, I decided to use my free time to teach myself game development.

  • Original Version (2020): The game was my third attempt at making a video game. I made the China level and started working on the Italy level, since the first Covid-19 cases in Iceland came from Italy, but I never finished the Italy level.
  • Remake (2025): Five years later, in a game development course at the University of Miami, I decided to finish it. I fixed the bugs and the messy code written by 15-year-old me, refined the gameplay, and added a new Iceland level to complete the project.
